Bridesmaid · Personal Belongings

Destinie Pann

  • Be Menelope's go-to person throughout the wedding.

  • Be responsible for Yada's personal belongings throughout the day.
  • Help Yada change for the after party with Moleda.

Bridesmaid · Hype Woman

Moleda Pann

♡ Good vibes + taking care of the girls.

  • Keep the bridal party energy fun, positive, and celebratory.
  • Check in on Yada and make sure the girls are eating and hydrated.
  • Make sure the bridal party has drinks when it's time to celebrate.
  • Be social, welcoming, and help guests feel included.
  • Help with simple guest questions; send logistics to Belle.
  • Help Yada change for the after party with Destinie.
Bridesmaid · Content + Beauty

Christine Thomas

♡ Capture the memories + keep Yada looking her best.

  • Capture candid photos/videos throughout getting ready, reception, and after party.
  • Know Yada's must-have shot list and keep the photographer/videographer aligned on key shots.
  • Help with the dress, train, veil, bustle, and finishing touches.
  • Be available for makeup touch-ups throughout the wedding and reception.
  • Keep tissues and touch-up essentials nearby for all the happy tears.
  • Do a quick appearance check before major moments and photos.
Bridesmaid · Yada's Right Hand

Jenny Mai

♡ Take care of Yada.

  • Stay close to Yada during the important parts of the day.
  • Keep Yada on schedule and give gentle time reminders.
  • Hold/manage Yada's phone when needed.
  • Make sure Yada eats, drinks water, and has what she needs.
  • Handle small issues before they reach Yada.
  • Redirect vendor/logistics questions to Belle.
  • Coordinate with the other bridesmaids when Yada needs something.
  • Make sure Yada is ready before major moments.
  • Protect Yada's peace — and make sure she actually has fun.
